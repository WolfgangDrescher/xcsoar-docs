XCSoar provides a full task management system, in which tasks can be edited
prior to flight and, when undertaking casual cross-country flying, modified
during flight.  Waypoints are advanced automatically or may be cycled through
manually. Many of XCSoar's computations relate to either turnpoints or the
finish waypoint. Unless a `true` task is defined, XCSoar will provide a `go
home` function, with many of the task related functions referring to the
location of takeoff. This chapter also describes the use of IGC loggers with
XCSoar.
